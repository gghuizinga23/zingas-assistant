# Zinga's Daily Schedule

Gerrit's rule (2026-10-30): this is the standard day for every design consultant at Zinga's. Gerrit works to it
too. He keeps it as a repeating list in his iPhone Reminders.

Use it as background knowledge only:
- To answer "what's left today?" or "what should I do now?".
- To put the items in a brief in the order Gerrit works them.
- To know where Gerrit is in his day before you write to him.

Do not send extra reminders because of this schedule. Do not tick anything off for Gerrit. If he asks you to remind
him of a block, ask him first which block and when.

## The day

A day has up to 4 appointments. After each appointment comes its post-appointment block, then a comm block, then
the next appointment.

1. 🌅 **Morning admin (7 to 9 AM)**
   - Emails from yesterday cleared.
   - All texts and calls returned.
   - Salesforce tasks and issues handled.
   - ALL estimates created and renamed.
   - Customers reviewed (a quick look at the day's customers).
2. 🏡 **Appointment (#1 to #4)**
   - Quote sent before he leaves the customer's home.
   - Revised if needed.
   - If sold: the customer signs on the iPad.
3. 🚗 **Post-appointment (#1 to #4). DO NOT MOVE.** He does it right after each appointment, before the next one.
   - If sold: submit the sale and turn in the check.
   - Result the appointment in RemodelerGo.
   - Update the Follow-Up Google Sheet.
   - Schedule the 48-hour follow-up text.
4. 📞 **Comm block (#1 to #4)**
   - Respond to new texts.
   - Return missed calls.
5. 🌙 **Night (8 to 9 PM)**
   - Respond to all emails.
   - Review each job. Gerrit does this with Grace, his wife.
   - Double-check accuracy.
   - Order ALL products sold today.
   - Quick prep for tomorrow.

## How your work fits the day
- The 48-hour follow-up text is your first follow-up after an estimate (skill `followup-cadence`). You draft it.
  Gerrit approves it. It is the same text, not a second one.
- Texts and missed calls come to the comm blocks. Have reply drafts ready before a comm block, so Gerrit only taps.
- Emails come to morning admin and the night block. Have Gmail drafts ready for them.
- An unresulted appointment, a missing Follow-Up Sheet line or an unordered sold job is a post-appointment or night
  step that did not happen. Say it plainly, in one line, when it is on the to-do list.
- Between appointments Gerrit reads you for five seconds. Keep it short.

No prices and no customer details go in anything you say about this schedule.
